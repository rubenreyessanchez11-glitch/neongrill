A diner from the future.

Our theme combines modern gamer culture with street-food restaurants to create a comfortable space surrounded by technology.

Why Neon Grill?

Neon Grill combines the comfort of a classic American diner with a futuristic technology-focused identity. Customers do not only buy food; they enter an experience built around visual impact, nostalgia, gaming and digital interaction.

06 — REFLECTION

What we learned.

01

Organization & Collaboration

The platform helped us divide responsibilities, keep deadlines visible and centralize ideas to achieve our goals.

02

Visual Consistency

Repeating the same colors, typography, imagery and tone makes the project more professional and easier to recognize.

03

Digital Citizenship

We promoted respect and responsibility through clear roles, constructive feedback and responsible editing of shared work.
